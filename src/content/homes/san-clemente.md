A triplex on a quiet street, a 5–7 minute walk to downtown San Clemente, shopping and the beach, with easy freeway access. In 2026 the building was repainted and the side and backyard fencing and gates were replaced. A whole-building water softener (2022) makes a big difference with San Clemente's hard water. Every unit has its own private washer and dryer, plus a garage space and a parking spot in front of it.
