A triplex on a quiet residential street within walking distance of downtown San Juan Capistrano. The Amtrak/Metrolink station is about five minutes away on foot, and the I-5 is a couple of minutes by car. Great trails are nearby, including the San Juan Creek bike path to Doheny Beach and the hiking, biking and horseback trails in the hills. Friendly, quiet neighbors make it a wonderful place to live.

Every unit has central heat, dual-pane vinyl windows, and its own one-car garage with storage and washer/dryer hookups, plus a parking spot in front of it. Guest street-parking permits are available from the city.
