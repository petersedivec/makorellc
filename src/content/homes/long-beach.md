A well-kept fourplex on a quiet street in Bixby Knolls, one of Long Beach's nicest neighborhoods. Trader Joe's is a two-minute walk, and ALDI, Ralphs, Whole Foods, Starbucks, Coffee Bean, lots of great local restaurants, banks and pharmacies are all within a few blocks. The 405 and 710 freeways are both about five minutes away, and Long Beach Airport is about ten.

The property has a large lawn, a beautiful shaded backyard and garden, evening lighting and off-street parking. Laundry is free: household washers and dryers (not coin-operated) are in the detached garage at the back. Tenants pay gas and electricity; we cover water, sewer, trash and landscaping. The whole property is non-smoking.

Each unit is about 860 sq ft, with front and back doors, lots of natural light and plenty of storage: two large hall closets, a living room closet, a linen closet, and about 6 feet of hanging space in each bedroom closet.
