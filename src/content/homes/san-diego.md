A charming 2-bed, 1-bath condo, completely remodeled with new windows throughout. The kitchen has high-end stainless Frigidaire Gallery appliances, quartz countertops and lots of storage, and the dining area has a built-in desk. Each bedroom is about 11' × 10', with 10 feet of closet and modular organizers you can rearrange. The hall has a linen closet and an in-unit washer/dryer combo that's handy for small loads.

The location is hard to beat. The Hub (Ralphs, Trader Joe's, shops and restaurants) is two blocks away, the heart of Hillcrest's restaurants, nightlife and Whole Foods a few blocks more, and the San Diego Zoo and Balboa Park less than a mile's walk. The condo includes one large reserved off-street parking space in front of the building. Residents share laundry and storage off the alley, plus a courtyard with a community BBQ.
