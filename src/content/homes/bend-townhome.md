Located in Bend's Midtown/Orchard District, this well-maintained Pahlisch-built townhome pairs low-maintenance living with a central, walkable location. It's a block from Orchard Park, with its playground, basketball court and Cascade views, and minutes from Albertsons, downtown, the Deschutes River and Midtown Yacht Club.

The open main level has a spacious great room with a gas fireplace, a kitchen with granite countertops, and a separate dining area. Upstairs, the primary suite has a walk-in closet and a double-vanity bath. Two more bedrooms and a full hall bath give you room for guests or a home office. There's generous closet space throughout, and the large two-car garage fits vehicles, gear and extra storage.

Other highlights include central A/C, forced-air gas heat, a large laundry room with new appliances, and a fenced, landscaped backyard with a patio. The HOA maintains the front landscaping, so you get the curb appeal without the upkeep. Built in 2014, the home's energy-efficient construction keeps utility costs low year-round.

It's one of Bend's most sought-after neighborhoods for walking and biking, close to downtown, the river, several parks, schools and St. Charles Medical Center.
