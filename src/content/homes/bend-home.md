A recently remodeled two-story home in an established neighborhood with easy access to all of Bend. Upstairs are three bedrooms and two full baths. The main level has a half bath, a walk-in pantry and a laundry room. The large three-car garage has extensive storage and a bonus office, and side parking by the backyard fits a large trailer or RV.

Recent updates include a new furnace and central A/C (2026), refinished kitchen cabinets, quartz countertops and backsplash, hardwood floors, a remodeled primary shower, an expanded Trex deck, a new retaining wall and fence, and updated light fixtures. Smaller touches make day-to-day living easier: closet systems in every bedroom and the pantry, a smart Wi-Fi thermostat, whole-house and garage fans for summer cooling, motion-sensor lights and USB outlets. The home comes with all major appliances (refrigerator, dishwasher, washer and dryer, and range), plus a second refrigerator in the garage.

Most errands take 10 minutes or less. Shevlin Park is about 10 minutes away, Mt. Bachelor about 30 minutes (25 miles), and the airport about 20 minutes. We lived here ourselves and found it a great spot for biking and running. Downtown dinners and concerts are an easy ride, and the Deschutes River Trail is just ¾ mile away. The corner market covers last-minute needs, and Albertsons and Natural Grocers are each about a mile away.
